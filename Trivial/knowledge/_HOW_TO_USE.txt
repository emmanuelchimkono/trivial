Put files here that you want the chatbot to know about: FAQs, product info,
notes, policies, a CV, anything.

- Supported types: .md, .txt, .pdf
- Files whose names start with an underscore (like this one) are ignored.
- The bot reads ALL of these files with every message, so keep them focused.
  A few dozen pages is fine. Hundreds of pages will be slow and costly.
- PDFs need real text in them. Scanned images of pages won't work.
- After adding or changing a file, push it to GitHub and the online app
  picks it up automatically.
